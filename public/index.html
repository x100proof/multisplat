How to play

Fights happen in rooms of up to 8 ninjas. Cut another ninja down to score a point. The scores for everyone are along the top. You respawn 2 seconds after dying.

Fighting

You have 100 HP. A chop does 34 damage and a sweep 25. For a moment after respawning you flash and can't be hurt, until you attack.

Long swords

A glowing blade sometimes appears in the arena. Walk into it to take it: your sword reaches further until you die. A ninja with a long sword carries a longer, glowing scabbard. When they are killed the sword drops where they fell, and anyone can take it. Slash a sword lying on the ground to smash it.

Ninja stars

Spinning stars appear often. Each pickup gives 5, 10 or 15 (the number floats over it; big bundles are rarer), and you can carry 30. A thrown star flies straight ahead and does 20 damage. The number you hold shows above your head and next to your HP. You lose them when you die.

Rooms

The first screen lists the open rooms (at most 10), each with its admin (★) and how many are fighting (of 8). Tap one to open it: pick a name and colour, then Fight or Watch. If it is full you wait in its queue. ← Rooms goes back to the list.

Create a room at the bottom of the list (the name is optional): you are its admin and land on its screen, where you can rename it and add or remove AI players (they take slots) before you press Fight. Going back to the list before anyone has joined closes it again. The admin also has the room settings (D, on a keyboard): gravity, jumps, speeds, damage, gore and the blood style, for everyone in the room until it closes. With the small Make admin button next to a player, or next to someone in the lobby of the room ("In the lobby"), you hand the role to someone else (it asks first); while others are playing in the room you must do that before you leave it. If the admin's connection is lost for good, the player who has been there longest takes over, else whoever has been looking at the room longest. A room closes when nobody is left in it or looking at it.

Watching

Press Watch in the lobby to see the fight without joining: no name needed, and it takes no slot, so it works even when the room is full. The camera follows the leader. Click a ninja to follow them, or use ← → to switch and ↓ to go back to the leader. Esc or the Lobby button takes you back to the lobby, where you can still press Fight.Tap a ninja to follow them, and the Leader button to go back to following the leader. The Lobby button or ✕ takes you back to the lobby, where you can still press Fight.

Privacy

To help fix problems, the server keeps the last hour of diagnostic logs from your browser (no keystrokes or chat text), with your IP address, your name and the id shown in the lobby.

The fight carries on while this is open.
