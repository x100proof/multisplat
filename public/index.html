How to play

Everyone fights in one arena. Cut another ninja down to score a point. The scores for everyone are along the top. You respawn 2 seconds after dying.

Fighting

You have 100 HP. A chop does 34 damage and a sweep 25. For a moment after respawning you flash and can't be hurt, until you attack.

Long swords

A glowing blade sometimes appears in the arena. Walk into it to take it: your sword reaches further until you die. A ninja with a long sword carries a longer, glowing scabbard. When they are killed the sword drops where they fell, and anyone can take it. Slash a sword lying on the ground to smash it.

Ninja stars

Spinning stars appear often. Each pickup gives 3, and you can carry 9. A thrown star flies straight ahead and does 20 damage. The number you hold shows above your head and next to your HP. You lose them when you die.

Watching

Press Watch in the lobby to see the fight without joining: no name needed, and it takes no slot, so it works even when the arena is full. The camera follows the leader. Click a ninja to follow them, or use ← → to switch and ↓ to go back to the leader. Esc or the Lobby button takes you back to the lobby, where you can still press Fight.Tap a ninja to follow them. Privacy

To help fix problems, the server keeps the last hour of diagnostic logs from your browser (no keystrokes or chat text), with your IP address, your name and the id shown in the lobby.

The fight carries on while this is open.

Your score will be lost.
